Evaluation model

Prove the request-to-PR path before expanding authority.

The commercial wedge is deliberately narrow: evaluate how DevBot handles a scoped engineering request, what evidence it produces, where human review intervenes, and whether the resulting change is acceptable.

Public truth boundary

What this evaluation does not promise.

DevBot is not marketed as a fully autonomous engineer. Security, production, performance, productivity, and cost claims remain evidence-gated, and higher-risk execution remains human-controlled.

NO

No bug-free guarantee

No silent production writes

The public evaluation surface does not grant deployment, merge, credential, infrastructure, or production-write authority.

NO

No invented ROI

Productivity, cycle-time, quality, and cost improvements require measured evaluation evidence before publication.
